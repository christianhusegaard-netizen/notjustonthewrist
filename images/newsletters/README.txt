NEWSLETTER PHOTOS — The Monthly Launch Edit

One folder per issue, named after the issue month: YYYY-MM (e.g. 2026-09, 2026-10).
Inside it, one photo per watch, named after the watch's number in the issue:

    01.jpg, 02.jpg, 03.jpg ... 10.jpg

- .jpg, .jpeg, .png and .webp all work (upper- or lower-case, e.g. .JPG). iPhone .HEIC photos do NOT show in browsers — export them as JPG first.
- Landscape photos look best (about 3:2). The whole watch is always shown, never cropped.
- Keep each file under about 500 KB so the page loads quickly.
- A missing photo is fine: that card simply shows without a picture.
- Use only photos you are allowed to use (your own, or official brand press images).

The September 2026 issue goes in:  images/newsletters/2026-09/
